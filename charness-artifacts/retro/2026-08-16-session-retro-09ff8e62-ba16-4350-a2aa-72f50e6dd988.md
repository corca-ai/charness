  Constrained: `quality_command` gets a warning and no teeth, because its executor
  aborts before publishing rather than half-mutating.
- **Blacklist → allowlist for the command recognizer.** Two rounds established that
  a metacharacter blacklist cannot be proven complete and that each miss is a *hard
  refusal of a working release*. Alternative skipped: adding the missing characters,
  which is the same rulebook one revision later. Constrained: the recognizer now
  answers `None` for more shapes, so it warns less and refuses correctly.
- **Disclose the pre-push guard's three exit-0 coverage holes rather than close
  them.** Making the creation cap or the stale remote-tracking exclusion a

  unpruned clone — how a push-time gate gets uninstalled. Recorded in the exit-code

- **Widen the shared close-keyword scanner despite blast radius.** Made safe enough

  commit messages. The two false-fire channels the widening opens are recorded with
  the command that re-measures them, not closed.
- **Stop at items 2, 5, 6 and one third of 7.** Items 3-remainder and 4 need design
  work with review budget behind it; starting either after four review rounds would

## Expert Counterfactuals

**Engelbart — (H + LAM + T) as one unit.** The tooling (T) here is strong: the
changed-line gate, the dup ratchet, the commit-msg floor and the bounded reviewers
all fired correctly and unprompted. The method-and-language (LAM) is what lagged.
`detector-blind-class-unstated` is a single lesson carrying two obligations —
*state the class* and *write the acceptance that fails on the wrong noun* — and a
human reading it under time pressure discharges the cheap half and reports the
lesson applied. Engelbart's move is not to write the lesson louder; it is to give
the second obligation its own T so LAM does not have to carry it. Concretely: the
`prove` stop gate should ask, for any slice touching a detector, "name one input
your acceptance would pass that your blind class says it must not judge" — a
question, not a gate, per P5. That converts a habit into a prompt at the moment of
use, which is the co-evolution the lens asks for.

**Ousterhout — deep modules, and the cost of a shallow interface.** He would have
pushed back on `command_script_target` before the first review round: a function
whose contract is "returns a path, or None, and None means two different things
(not-judged vs cannot-exist)" is a shallow interface leaking its implementation.
The whole quoted/tilde/glob/metacharacter saga is that leak being paid for twice.
His counterfactual: make the return type carry the distinction —
`Judged(path) | NotJudged(reason)` — and the caller's refusal branch becomes
unwriteable for the not-judged case, which is precisely the false-refusal class
both rounds found. The evidence he would have demanded before the first commit:
one enumerated list of *what the caller does with each return value*, which would
have exposed that `bump_version` turns the ambiguous `None`-adjacent case into a
`SystemExit`.
