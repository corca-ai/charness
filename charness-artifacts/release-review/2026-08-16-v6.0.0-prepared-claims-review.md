## Why this is a different subject from the stopped review

The earlier `2026-08-16-v6.0.0-claims-review.md` stopped at `unproven` after three
rounds over a body that would have been posted as ten irreversible GitHub close
comments. That body is NOT in this payload: this publication omits `--close-issue`
entirely, so the release closes no issue and the disputed per-issue claims are not
carried anywhere. The issue closes were separated from the release by owner decision
and remain unperformed. This review therefore reads the release RECORD, not the close
claims, and is a first review of that subject rather than a fourth round on the old one.

## What the rounds found

Round 1 returned `unproven` on one blocking false claim, and it was authored in this
range: the notes asserted that every gate now captures its status first and warns on a
failed removal. Only `run-quality.sh` and `check-markdown.sh` do. The other four
sibling gates received only the guard that stops a failed removal aborting the trap,
and they discard the error — so the sentence's own rationale was asserted over gates
that throw that signal away. Round 1 also found the calibration pointing at an artifact
carrying none of it, three missing disclaimers, a present-tense description of a
carrier that had since been repaired, and a glossed trigger difference.

Round 2 read the repairs and returned `pass` with two non-blocking findings, both on
sentences round 1's repair had introduced: "fork nothing" was false (all four of those
gates fork; three fork into the path their trap removes), and "the module docstring is
the full list" over-claimed.

Round 3 read the repairs to those, on the exact prepared bytes, and returned `pass`.
It verified the corrected sentence literally — enumerating every fork in the four
scripts and confirming that the only backgrounded children in `scripts/check-*.sh` are
`check-markdown.sh`'s two, which is precisely the property the sentence now asserts.

## Accepted, not repaired

Round 3 left two should-fix findings on one sentence, and they ship as-is deliberately.
The sentence sends a reader to "the module docstrings" of both guard modules, while the
stale-clone caveat it names actually lives in a FUNCTION docstring (`range_commits`) in
the scan module; and the notes do not disclose that a malformed pre-push stdin yields
exit 0 having judged nothing. Neither is a false statement about behavior. They are not
repaired because round 3's own closing advice was that a third repair to this sentence
should be read by someone before it ships, and making a fourth unread edit to the bytes
under review would defeat the review that just passed. They are recorded here and in the
handoff instead.
