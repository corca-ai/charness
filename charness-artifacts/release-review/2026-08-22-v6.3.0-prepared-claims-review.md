# Charness 6.3.0 Prepared Claims Review

Date: 2026-08-22

## Verdict

UNPROVEN through round 3. Three independent claims-review rounds ran against
three successive prepared records and all three returned `unproven`. Nothing is
published: no tag exists, nothing is pushed, and each round's prepared commit was
reset before the next was built. A fourth round is running against the repairs
below; this record exists because three rounds left no durable trace and the
repo keeps one per release.

## Why This Record Exists

`charness-artifacts/release-review/` holds a per-release claims artifact for
every release from v5.1.0 forward, including `2026-08-16-v6.0.0-claims-review.md`,
which records an UNPROVEN outcome. v6.3.0 had none. Three rounds that each reset
a prepared commit survived only as prose inside the goal and retro — the same two
artifacts every round found to be inaccurate. An auditor reading the release
record's `## Claims Review` field ("not yet performed") would have seen no trace
of them at all.

## The Rounds

Each round read the prepared record, the release notes, the critique, the goal
artifact and the retro, with Read/Grep/Glob only and no exec tool.

### Round 1 — 3 blockers

Record defects, the sharpest of which was the goal artifact's own staleness: its
Slice Log still said slice A's round 2 was "owed and running" when it had
completed, frozen into a tree about to be published.

### Round 2 — 3 blockers

1. The quality sentence was still a hardcoded literal on the writer that
   produces the record pushed to `main` (`commit_post_publish_artifact`). Round
   1's repair had patched two call sites and missed it.
2. Slice A's Slice Log said one review round while the Slice Plan said two.
3. Two false `applied:` dispositions in the retro, each naming a destination the
   repair had not reached.

### Round 3 — 4 blockers

Every one of them a round-2 repair carrying the class it repaired:

1. **B1** — of round 2's two false `applied:` dispositions, one was repaired and
   one was not. The issue-closure bullet still claimed a deferral was "recorded
   in the goal's Coordination Cues"; that section contained only scaffold prose
   and two unfilled placeholders.
2. **B2** — the disposition for the hardcoded-quality-literal finding credited
   `publish_release_execute.py` with rendering the measured value. That is the
   call-site mechanism round 2 REJECTED, and the helper it described had been
   deleted. The disposition for the hardcoded-literal finding was itself a
   hardcoded claim that had stopped being true.
3. **B3** — slice A's record swapped one internal contradiction for another:
   `23642a769` was called "round-2 repairs" on one line and round 1's repairs
   two lines down, and the actual round-2 commit appeared nowhere in the tree.
   Round 2's four findings were recorded nowhere.
4. **B4** — the corrected counts omitted an entire claims round ("the first
   returned unproven" alongside "two claims rounds") and carried an unreconciled
   "nine" after the total had changed.

Round 3 also confirmed what the rounds were for: the quality-sentence mechanism
is genuinely fixed at the owner, and every version surface and derived figure
reconciles.

## What Was Repaired After Round 3

Facts were re-derived from `git log` / `git show` rather than from the prose that
had been wrong three times — a different evidence channel, which is the specific
reason a fourth round is being run rather than the release being stopped here.

- `## Coordination Cues` filled with real routing, a `Gather: n/a` opt-out, the
  release evidence, and an explicit `Issue closeout: DEFERRED` entry naming the
  four open issues, the absent carrier, and the floor that was not run — which
  makes B1's disposition true rather than downgrading it.
- The quality-sentence disposition rewritten to name the owner
  (`publish_release_artifact.write_current_artifact`) and its structural test,
  and to state that the earlier draft credited the rejected repair.
- Slice A's commits re-attributed from git: `99c440aa7` implementation,
  `23642a769` round 1, `96ba78f7f` round 2. Round 2's four findings written into
  the Slice Log. Slice C's omitted round-2 commit added.
- Counts corrected to three claims rounds, eleven spawns, twenty-one blockers,
  eight of them repairs carrying their own class.
- The release critique's narrower scope disclosed in the notes: it read 9
  commits / 57 paths against a 78-path delta, and `96ba78f7f` is verdict-logic
  that post-dates it.
