## The three blocking findings, and their repairs

1. **The notes named a stale path count.** The review-scope bullet said the
   record "evaluates `79` paths"; the record says `117` and the real delta is
   `118`. `79` was carried forward from round 4. The bullet exists to disclose
   how much of the shipped delta the release critique never covered, so the
   error inverted its purpose: it disclosed a 22-path gap where the real gap is
   60. Its bolded headline also contradicted its own body. Repaired, and the
   headline now says about half the delta was never critiqued.

2. **"no existing artifact reddens on update" was false.** Both premises hold —
   exit-code reach is `--pursue-ready` only, and non-shaping statuses skip — but
   `draft` IS a shaping status and the hollow floor has no date grandfather,
   unlike the backlog floor beside it. A pre-existing draft whose shaping
   sections are empty or scaffold-identical passed before and exits 1 now. That
   is the reported defect, and the report came from a consumer repo, so
   artifacts in that state exist. The sentence also refuted itself by offering a
   per-artifact remedy for reddening it had just denied. The bump rationale and
   the notes now state the real blast radius.

3. **"no consuming repo authors a claims record" was false.** The whole release
   skill ships in the checked-in export, and the prepared stop is unconditional,
   so a consumer who updates and resumes a prepared release must author a v3
   record. The rationale now says so and argues the level on additivity instead
   of on a denial its own shipped code refutes.
