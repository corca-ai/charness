### Quality and changed-line mutation claim

The record names the exact adapter command
`./scripts/run-quality.sh --release --non-claim=release-changed-line-coverage`
and reports exit 0 in 131.8 seconds at `post-bump, pre-commit`, with the
remaining configured release gates active. The release notes state the same

result. The record does not claim hosted mutation success.

The earlier no-flag changed-line run's 34-file uncovered result and the
dirty-worktree refusal are evidence for why the lane is omitted, not evidence
that the omitted lane passed. The prepared record's quality sentence is
therefore honest as a non-claim, subject to the release helper re-running its
declared command on resume.

explicitly says it is not empirical consumer-development speed data. This
