# Resolution Brief — corca-ai/charness#812

**Classification**: feature

**Reporter JTBD**: During the early `achieve`/`ideation`/`spec` planning ping-pong, the agent should proactively recommend the next learning move—even when the operator does not ask for a reality check—so a direction-invalidating assumption meets a cheap sufficiently real probe before it hardens into the Goal Draft or implementation contract.

**Proposed boundary**:

- in scope: define one early-planning routing sequence consumed by `achieve`, `ideation`, and `spec`; make each entry point assess planning maturity and recommend the next move (`stay in ideation`, `run a bounded reality probe`, `shape the spec`, or `return to ideation`); test that route against the Ceal episode and a contrasting completed rework case; retain existing owners for diagnosis and proof.
- out of scope: Ceal-specific CLI rules, a universal packet for routine work, mandatory fresh-agent or live-provider acceptance for every task, and static enforcement of semantic judgments such as whether two fields mean the same thing.

**Non-goals**:

- Do not add the same checklist to nine skill bodies.
- Do not require the operator to know which of `achieve`, `ideation`, or `spec` should be invoked next.
- Do not create another lifecycle orchestrator or evidence schema before a thin worked-example probe shows that one is necessary.
- Do not convert incident history directly into one regression test per event.
- Do not treat deterministic replay as proof of fresh-agent effectiveness.

**Acceptance checks**:

- On a consequential sample, the highest-risk assumption and the cheapest sufficiently real falsifier are visible before broad implementation.
- Entering through any of `achieve`, `ideation`, or `spec` produces a concrete recommended next planning move without the operator having to mention assumption testing or the sequence by name.
- `achieve` does not freeze or seek approval for a plan whose direction-invalidating assumption is merely listed; it recommends the owning ideation or probe move and states what observation would permit planning to continue.
- `ideation` recommends a reality probe when conversation cannot honestly settle the riskiest assumption, and recommends `spec` only when the concept and its largest assumptions are sufficiently stable.
- `spec` routes an existential concept or user-path uncertainty back to ideation/probe instead of disguising it as an ordinary implementation `Probe Question`; bounded mechanism uncertainty remains probe-friendly inside the spec.
- An observed failure is recorded separately from its interpretation, and an ownership/derivation/boundary/proof diagnosis precedes mechanism selection.
- A scenario-passing repair that duplicates a source of truth is explicitly challenged, while a deletion or derivation repair remains easy to choose.
- Structural checks and actual user/model/provider acceptance carry separate claims and non-claims.
- A routine reversible task can still proceed without a new packet, extra agent, or heavy acceptance run.
- Each normative concept has one canonical owner; consuming skills link or contribute a local transition instead of copying the whole loop.

**Open decisions**:

- Canonical shape: extend the existing shared generative-sequence reference with an early-planning route, or create a narrower shared planning-route reference consumed by all three skills.
- Engagement threshold: identify a direction-invalidating assumption by expected rework amplification across code/tests/docs/operator surfaces, without turning every unresolved detail into an early reality probe.
- Handoff behavior: decide whether the skills only recommend the next named move or also directly continue into that skill when no consequential operator decision is open.
- Durable record: keep assumption and probe state in the existing Goal Draft/ideation/spec artifacts and define only a small handoff vocabulary, rather than introducing a new shared mutable packet.
- Enforcement: keep route selection judgment-led while adding focused scenario acceptance that proves the three entry points make the expected recommendation.
- Pilot: use the Ceal migration plus one contrasting historical rework case, and decide which contrasting case best tests that the design is not CLI-specific.

**Autonomous vs pause**: pausing for user discussion because the canonical owner, engagement threshold, durable record, enforcement boundary, and pilot comparison materially change the solution.
