# Resolution Brief — corca-ai/charness#812

**Classification**: feature

**Reporter JTBD**: During Achieve's existing orchestration of early `ideation` and `spec` work, the agent should proactively choose the next learning move—even when the operator does not ask for a reality check—so a direction-invalidating assumption meets a cheap sufficiently real probe before it hardens into the Goal Draft or implementation contract.

**Proposed boundary**:

- in scope: strengthen Achieve's existing coordination policy so its planning phase selects and sequences `ideation`, a bounded reality probe, and `spec` from current uncertainty; define what evidence or disposition crosses each handoff; test the orchestration against the Ceal episode and a contrasting completed rework case; retain each called skill's existing concept, diagnosis, and proof ownership.
- out of scope: Ceal-specific CLI rules, a universal packet for routine work, mandatory fresh-agent or live-provider acceptance for every task, and static enforcement of semantic judgments such as whether two fields mean the same thing.

**Non-goals**:

- Do not add the same checklist to nine skill bodies.
- Do not create a second router beside Achieve or require the operator to manually invoke each coordinated skill.
- Do not create another lifecycle orchestrator or evidence schema before a thin worked-example probe shows that one is necessary.
- Do not convert incident history directly into one regression test per event.
- Do not treat deterministic replay as proof of fresh-agent effectiveness.

**Acceptance checks**:

- On a consequential sample, the highest-risk assumption and the cheapest sufficiently real falsifier are visible before broad implementation.
- An ordinary Achieve start selects and invokes the appropriate next planning owner without the operator having to mention assumption testing or manually drive the `ideation`/`spec` ping-pong.
- `achieve` does not freeze or seek approval for a plan whose direction-invalidating assumption is merely listed; it invokes the owning ideation or probe move and records what observation would permit planning to continue.
- The ideation result makes the riskiest assumption and any needed reality probe consumable by Achieve; Achieve invokes `spec` only when the concept and its largest assumptions are sufficiently stable or explicitly dispositioned.
- When spec work exposes an existential concept or user-path uncertainty, Achieve routes it back through ideation/probe instead of letting it remain an ordinary implementation `Probe Question`; bounded mechanism uncertainty remains probe-friendly inside the spec.
- An observed failure is recorded separately from its interpretation, and an ownership/derivation/boundary/proof diagnosis precedes mechanism selection.
- A scenario-passing repair that duplicates a source of truth is explicitly challenged, while a deletion or derivation repair remains easy to choose.
- Structural checks and actual user/model/provider acceptance carry separate claims and non-claims.
- A routine reversible task can still proceed without a new packet, extra agent, or heavy acceptance run.
- Each normative concept has one canonical owner; consuming skills link or contribute a local transition instead of copying the whole loop.

**Open decisions**:

- Canonical shape: place the selection/sequence policy in Achieve's existing coordination surface, with only reusable learning-order principles in the shared generative-sequence reference.
- Engagement threshold: identify a direction-invalidating assumption by expected rework amplification across code/tests/docs/operator surfaces, without turning every unresolved detail into an early reality probe.
- Handoff behavior: decide when Achieve continues directly into the selected skill/probe and when cost, external effect, or a consequential operator decision requires a pause.
- Durable record: keep assumption and probe state in the existing Goal Draft/ideation/spec artifacts and define only a small handoff vocabulary, rather than introducing a new shared mutable packet.
- Enforcement: keep orchestration judgment-led while adding focused Achieve scenario acceptance that proves the existing orchestrator selects the expected next owner without an operator naming it.
- Pilot: use the Ceal migration plus one contrasting historical rework case, and decide which contrasting case best tests that the design is not CLI-specific.

**Autonomous vs pause**: pausing for user discussion because the canonical owner, engagement threshold, durable record, enforcement boundary, and pilot comparison materially change the solution.
