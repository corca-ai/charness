## Counterweight Pass

Most declared floors are genuinely discharged with real captured results, and the
floor audit said so plainly: the standalone critique artifact, the second bounded
round on the #608 verdict-logic repair, the rung-1 presence floor, the durable
observer probe, fresh-checkout probes (a real executed result, not configuration),
the maintainer install refresh, the installed version/doctor readback, evergreen
update instructions, the real-host trigger evaluation, the published-notes audit,
and the `not_requested` issue-closeout default. The blockers are not a general
indictment of the release.

Three reviewer positions were tested and did not survive intact:

- The claims reviewer's extension of the stale hosted-CI non-claim — that green
  default-branch CI *is* "the direct-to-default readback multiple ledger rows are
  waiting on" — is **rejected**. Per
  `skills/public/issue/references/closeout-discipline.md:91-104`,
  `direct-to-default` names a *carrier*: a default-branch commit whose body
  carries close keywords plus the classification ledger, followed by
  `verify-closeout --carrier direct-commit --commit-ref <ref> --expect-state CLOSED`.
  Hosted CI does not discharge it. Adopting the extension would have closed
  eleven cohort issues on a readback that never happened.
- Claims O2 (hash binding) and O6 (published body) were raised as unresolved and
  are **resolved clean** by parent-run evidence below; neither is a defect.
- The floors reviewer's B1 is real as an *evidentiary* finding but must not be
  overstated: it does not establish fabrication. Recorded below as unproven.
