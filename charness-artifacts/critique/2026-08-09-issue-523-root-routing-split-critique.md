  routes only consumer hook configuration to that reference.
- External boundaries retain standing issue creation, conditional issue close,
  per-phase push/tag/version/release/Cautilus grants, grant revocation on weaker
  proof, and distinct-channel readback.
- The protected delegation block is exactly 4,151 bytes with identical SHA-256
  before and after. `CLAUDE.md -> AGENTS.md` remains intact.

## Counterweight Pass

- Act Before Ship: weakened precedence and wrong-scope failure-log ownership
  could change behavior for a first-session reader; both are repaired.
- Keep: the operating-contract addition is cohesive with commit/closeout
  discipline and does not duplicate the root summary.
- Over-Worry: matching a sibling repo's 1KB root would require deleting the
  explicitly protected delegation authorization; size imitation is not the goal.
- Bundle Anyway: root-specific assertions pin the two repaired summaries without
  adding a general prompt-size floor.
