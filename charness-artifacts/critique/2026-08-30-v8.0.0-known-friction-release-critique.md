## Failure Angles

- Operability: a fresh clone initially lacked generated plugin mirrors, so a
  publish-current run could refuse even though canonical packaging said 8.0.0.
- Irreversible-boundary capability: `e3d7aeef0` deleted the pre-publish
  real-host detector and its manual checklist with no replacement; the decision
  needed a fresh review of the retained and post-publish channels.
- Narrative accuracy: the abandoned v8 notes had a stale derived gate count and
  framed benefit too close to an empirical speed claim.
- Consumer-value scope: closed/not-planned trackers can remain known friction;
  issue state must not be translated into "all friction removed."
- Ownership leakage: consumer Git, submodule, worktree, and topology composition
  could be pulled back into Charness despite the explicit capability boundary.
- Count substitution: line, test-ratio, and migration-completeness asks could be
  mistaken for consumer JTBD evidence.

## Counterweight Pass

The generated-surface absence, stale notes, missing critique carrier, and
unreviewed real-host removal were real blockers in the first review. Canonical
sync removed version drift; the new notes pass derived-claim and narrative
checks; this artifact supplies the standalone carrier; and a separate Luna
review covered the removal against the retained controls.

Restoring the former broad real-host checklist is not required. Its recorded
triggers fired on common paths that did not change install behavior. The current
release contract preserves fresh-checkout probes, required surface checks,
distinct claims review, public observation, and explicit post-publication
`update`/`version`/`doctor` readbacks. The trade is now disclosed plainly: those
installed-host observations detect a bad release after publication and do not
prevent publication. Pinning `v7.0.0` is the documented rollback.

The known-friction reviewer correctly found that "all friction removed" would
overclaim and highlighted issue #715. Its description of #717 as missing
one-command packet assembly is disconfirmed by
`charness-artifacts/goal-runs/724/bodies/backlog-717.md`: #717 concerns internal
Goal readiness proof headroom and is not consumer packet convenience. It is not
carried into the release claim.
