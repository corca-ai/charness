# Release Critique — 3.1.0 → 3.1.1

Date: 2026-08-07

## Decision Under Review

Publish a `patch` bump to **3.1.1** covering one defect and its fallout: a truncated
read of a gate's output lost the only fact the reader could act on. `run-quality.sh`'s
last line reported a COUNT and not a NAME, and the last line is the one line every
truncation preserves.

## Failure Angles

- `patch` is dishonest because an output FORMAT changed and something parses it.
- Three existing assertions were hand-edited to match new output — sanding, not updating.
- The new failure-log directory leaks, grows, or misdirects with stale content.
- The new bash breaks under the runner's phase concurrency or on an odd label.
- Version drift or an unsynced mirror at publish time.

## Counterweight Pass

Four angles cleared with evidence. One produced a pre-publish blocker, and it was the
same class the release exists to remove — a claim made unconditionally while the thing
backing it could silently fail.
