## Goal

Repair the producer-boundary pattern through one coordinated goal containing two
consumer repairs: #515's quality-surface routing/disclosure and #514's
closeout-evidence assembly. First qualify whether a narrow, immutable common
projection exists at the existing producer/consumer seams. Only if at least two
actual readers consume identical field semantics, with one canonical projection
producer per field and source-fact ownership separately mapped, may the goal add
a common projection containing `projection_owner` (the field-level owner of
serialization, versioning, refusal, and repair), `execution_root`, `identity`,
`locator_policy`, `final_consumer`, and `unexamined_axes`; otherwise it must
continue with two consumer-local repairs and an explicit no-shared-artifact
composition record. In both branches, use existing owners, replay the recorded
#517/#516 fixtures, and close their Charness-owned issue boundaries independently with their own carrier,
delegated critique, distinct behavior verdict, and GitHub readback. If the
crosswalk proves that a required #515 acceptance belongs only to the consumer
repo, record that external-owner boundary and do not claim this goal complete
until that boundary is separately resolved or explicitly re-scoped by the user.
