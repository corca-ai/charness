## Goal

Repair the producer-boundary pattern through one coordinated goal containing
three independent issue repairs: #518's quality declaration/preset/surface
reconciliation, #515's quality-surface routing/disclosure, and #514's
closeout-evidence assembly. The shared pattern is `declared intent → resolved
reader → applicability/scope → observed evidence → final verdict`; any missing,
unreachable, unsupported, deferred, or failed edge must remain visible and must
not collapse into clean/green. First qualify whether a narrow, immutable common
projection exists at the existing producer/consumer seams. Only if at least two
actual readers consume identical field semantics, with one canonical projection
producer per field and source-fact ownership separately mapped, may the goal add
a common projection containing `projection_owner` (the field-level owner of
serialization, versioning, refusal, and repair), `execution_root`, `identity`,
`locator_policy`, `final_consumer`, and `unexamined_axes`; otherwise it must
continue with three consumer-local repairs and an explicit no-shared-artifact
composition record. In both branches, use existing owners, replay the recorded
#517/#516/#511/#507 fixtures, and close #518, #515, and #514 independently with
their own carrier, delegated critique, distinct behavior verdict, and GitHub
readback. If the crosswalk proves that a required acceptance belongs only to a
consumer repo, record that external-owner boundary and do not claim this goal
complete until that boundary is separately resolved or explicitly re-scoped by
the user.
