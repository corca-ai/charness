## Goal

Compare session-bound agent score observations across sessions and against the
unchanged preview, then decide whether a specific score-policy problem exists
and whether a narrowly reviewed policy change is warranted.

## Non-Goals

- Manufacturing zero or negative scores, choosing an arbitrary cohort size, or
  adding a budget merely because scores are nonzero.
- Claiming presentation receipt, human usefulness, calibration, contract
  graduation, release, or external behavior.

  sign diversity, and comparative no-change/proposed-policy preview
