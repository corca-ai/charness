# Long-term delegation usefulness must be observed separately at each model tier

## Capability and fixed decisions

Feature; dependencies: comparison-contract, closeout-carrier, release-reuse, context-reuse at local proof readiness. Execute the frozen neutral four-arm protocol only with evaluator authority. Candidate is a named, packaged integrated revision; no-plugin receives equivalent ordinary tools and semantic context, without Charness treatment machinery.

## Execution and acceptance

- Re-verify frozen input/settings identities and contamination checks before each arm. No arm inherits another arm's outputs, caches or comments. Use scheduled checkpoint/context replacement/delta exactly as frozen.
- Observe final behavior outside producer context. Report each acceptance predicate, unchanged behavior and delta handling, forbidden mutations, first correct resume action and any rescue event. Scheduled experimental events do not count as user rescue.
- Retain phase and total elapsed time, available tokens, separate evaluator/provider overhead, rework events and sourced cost if observable. Necessary fresh resume reads, changed-input verification and independent observation are not redundant work.
- Preserve every original arm, including failure. A setup failure is invalid/unexecuted, not zero-cost success; a task failure is not an infrastructure retry. Apply the frozen retry policy and report exclusions.
- Report both model-tier comparisons, even if plugin loses or only one benefits. Single-scenario results are directional, not general reliability or statistical superiority.

## Bounded repair and stopping rule

A finding may justify a repair in the already-authorized changed seam. Preserve initial results and candidate hash; repaired-candidate reruns are separately labelled. Do not tune the oracle/task to make the candidate win. A changed oracle requires a new comparison, not replacement rows. Stop after the frozen pilot plus at most one separately identified repair validation round; broader replication or a new product direction requires operator direction.

## Ownership and non-goals

Neutral experiment orchestration owns isolation/stimulus/logging, producer owns its task, independent observer owns actual acceptance, parent owns interpretation and revision-to-release equivalence. No external repository creation, new maintained runner, or result dashboard. Publication child binds observations to shipped bytes or labels them pre-release-only.
