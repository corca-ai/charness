# Release resume repeats semantic verification after a sealed prepare

## Problem and capability

Goal #798's prepare and resume repeated the release quality command, with the latter taking 383.362 seconds. Claims/evidence changed between stages, so this is a candidate dominated execution rather than proof that every second check is unnecessary. Reuse unchanged subject evidence across eligible claims-only changes while retaining current publication safeguards.

## Fixed decisions and ownership

- Feature; no dependencies. Release owns eligibility and handoff; existing quality/pre-push receipt owners retain their verification semantics. Do not introduce a universal cache or broaden skip flags.
- Name equivalence before implementation: subject source and dependency closure, verifier bytes/configuration, command scope, runtime/tool versions, materialized export roots, receipt provenance and allowed metadata delta. Commit identity alone is insufficient.
- Missing or invalid equivalence never yields reuse. It follows an explicit full re-verification or refuses with the actionable reason; no hidden partial green.

## First action and probes

Inspect prepare/resume/common sealing and pre-push consumers under `skills/public/release/scripts/` and `.githooks/pre-push`. Classify inputs into semantic subject, proof/verifier, and review/audit-only bytes, including inventory regenerated by gates. Freeze the actual closure and delta allow-rule with independent review before removing a call. Treat dynamic/unknown inputs conservatively; record the fallback rather than inventing equality.

## Success criteria and checks

- Eligible prepare-to-resume invokes the semantic subject suite once; receipt construction, eligibility check and required current boundary checks remain observed separately. Capture real before/after invocations and added overhead.
- Each changed identity axis, corrupted/missing receipt, changed materialized root, adversarial file disguised as claims-only data, and stale/foreign prepared lineage prevents reuse at the final pre-push/publication consumer.
- Version drift, authorization, claims eligibility, fresh checkout/package, public readback and installed behavior remain current and independently observed. Reuse is not publication approval.
- Tests prove both eligible and fallback/refusal branches, including changes between prepare and resume. Fresh-eye review examines the invariant and a distinct input-axis counterexample; verdict-changing repairs follow the bounded review cap.

## Non-goals and deferred choices

No removal merely to meet a time target; no general filesystem cache or relaxed unknown-input policy. If the closure cannot be made trustworthy and cheaper within this seam, record A2 unmet and seek a scoped disposition, not a false completion. Parent owns release authorization, integration and final publication.
